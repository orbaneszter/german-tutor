B1 to C2 · grammar diagnostic for the C2 (GDS) study plan · works offline, nothing is sent anywhere

How it works

  1. Part 1, grammar (35–45 min, here): 72 questions, 3 for each grammar unit of the curriculum, from B2 up to C2. Answer without looking anything up.
  2. Part 2, reading and listening (about 2 hours, separately): the Lesen (65 min, pages 7–15) and Hören (about 40 min, pages 17–21, with the MP3s from goethe.de) modules of c1-modular_modellsatz.pdf. Don't look past page 28 until you're done: the answer keys and the transcripts come after it. Score them with the answer key and enter your percentages on the results page. Don't use the C2 practice set; save it for your first full mock exam.
  3. Part 3, writing (45 min, here): one short argumentative text, rated with the rubric prompt this page gives you.

Choose "Ich weiß es nicht" whenever you would be guessing. A lucky guess puts you on a faster track than you're ready for. If the questions get too hard, the test offers you a way to stop early.

Your answers are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back later.

Keyboard: 1–4 to answer, 5 for "Ich weiß es nicht".
